The Problem

48% of people
miss medication
doses regularly.

And most apps make it worse — not better.

🏥

Existing apps feel clinical and overwhelming

Dense UI, medical jargon, and feature overload turn a simple daily habit into a chore. People stop using them within a week.

🔔

Reminders without context create anxiety, not action

A generic push notification at 8am doesn't tell you what to take, how much, or whether you already took it. It just stresses you out.

Design Challenge

"How might we design a medication tracker that feels like a personal companion — not a medical device?"

Feature 01

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

The dashboard was designed around a single moment — opening the app and knowing exactly what to do next. No clutter. Just your pills, your times, and a satisfying check.

  • ⏰
    Time-sorted dose schedule — Morning to evening, in order. No hunting.
  • ✓
    One-tap dose logging — Check it off. Strikethrough confirms it. Done.
  • 🌅
    Personalised greeting — Small touch. Big difference in how the app feels.

Feature 02

Add once.
Track forever.

Each pill gets its own colour, schedule, and personality. The colour system makes it instantly recognisable at a glance — no reading required. Recognition over recall.

  • 🎨
    Colour-coded pills — Yellow, blue, violet, green. Each one unique and memorable.
  • 🗓️
    Flexible scheduling — Once daily, twice daily, specific days. It adapts to your regimen.
  • ✏️
    Edit any time — Dosage changed? New timing? Update in seconds.

Feature 03

Reminders that respect your time.

Push notifications arrive at exactly the right moment — not before, not after. The schedule view gives you a clear time-based layout so you're always one step ahead.

  • 🕐
    Time-blocked view — Morning, afternoon, evening. Grouped by when, not by name.
  • 📆
    Weekly awareness — See your whole week at a glance. Know what's coming before it arrives.
  • 🔕
    Smart notifications — Triggered by browser APIs. No backend. No data collection.

Feature 04

Know before you run out.
Stay on a roll.

Stock tracking predicts when you'll run out based on your dose frequency. Streaks turn consistency into a game — because habits are built through repetition, not willpower.

Design Decisions

Intentional by default.

Every choice has a reason. Here are the three that shaped everything.

01

Dark mode first

Most pills are taken morning and evening. A dark UI respects the context of use — it doesn't blast your eyes at 7am or disturb your sleep routine at night.

02

Colour-coded pills

Colour removes cognitive load. You shouldn't have to read the name of your pill — you should recognise it. Recognition beats recall, especially when you're half-awake.

03

No backend

Privacy by design. All data lives on your device. localStorage means zero accounts, zero data collection, zero risk of a data breach. Simple is safer.

Tech Stack

Built lean. Built right.

Every tool was chosen deliberately — nothing added for the sake of it.

Reflection

What I'd do differently.

Honest notes from the other side of shipping.

1

Add data export from day one

Users asked for backup and sync almost immediately. I'd treat data portability as a core feature, not an afterthought. A simple JSON export would have covered 90% of the need.

2

Shorten the onboarding flow

The first-run experience had five steps. Three is the sweet spot. People want to see their first pill on the dashboard before they trust the app — so get them there faster.
