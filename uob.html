The Environment

About the platform

UOB Infinity is a corporate digital banking platform supporting Corporate Cash Management, Financial Supply Chain Management, and Trade workflows across multiple markets.

As part of an in-house UX team, I worked on improving existing experiences within a highly regulated banking environment where usability, operational requirements, security, and technical constraints all needed to coexist.

The work involved designing for complex transaction flows, internal operational tools, and continuous product improvements across live enterprise systems.

My Role

Embedded in the team

Embedded within an 8-person design team, I collaborated closely with Product Owners, Product Managers, Business Analysts, engineers, System Analysts, and stakeholders across multiple initiatives.

Working in an agile environment, I contributed across the end-to-end product lifecycle — from problem framing and flow ideation to refinement, handoff, and delivery support.

My work focused on improving usability, strengthening design consistency, and supporting scalable product experiences across the platform.

How I Work

My approach

01 — Agile Delivery

Working iteratively, shipping continuously

Worked within agile product teams to deliver iterative UX improvements aligned with sprint timelines, business priorities, and technical feasibility. Comfortable moving fast while keeping quality high.

02 — End-to-End Product Thinking

From problem framing to delivery

Contributed across the full design process — from identifying problems and refining flows to supporting implementation and delivery within a live enterprise product. Never just a screen-pusher.

03 — Systems & Security Awareness

Designing within constraints

Designed within a highly regulated banking environment, balancing usability with operational, technical, compliance, and security requirements. Constraints aren't blockers — they're part of the brief.

04 — Cross-Functional Collaboration

Aligned across every team

Partnered closely with product, engineering, and business teams to align design decisions through structured communication, systems thinking, and shared problem-solving. Design as a shared language.

01 / 04

Improving Cross-Border Payment Workflows

UOB Pay / UOB Send

Problem

Corporate payment flows involved complex transaction requirements, making it challenging for users to complete actions quickly and confidently across multiple markets.

What I Did

Improved key payment interactions and refined transaction flows to make the experience clearer, more intuitive, and better aligned with regulatory and operational requirements.

Outcome

Strengthened usability and consistency across payment workflows while supporting smoother transaction experiences for corporate users.

02 / 04

Designing Sub-Account Management

Virtual Account Sub-Account (VASA)

Problem

Users needed a clearer way to create and manage sub-accounts within complex organisational account structures.

What I Did

Designed and refined the sub-account creation experience, focusing on simplifying account hierarchy visibility and improving overall usability for financial administration tasks.

Outcome

Created a more structured and manageable experience for users handling corporate account organisation and management.

03 / 04

Rapid UX Improvements & Business Requests

Agile Product Enhancements

Problem

Business and operational teams frequently required urgent product updates and UX changes within short delivery timelines.

What I Did

Collaborated closely with product and engineering teams to quickly assess requirements, propose feasible UX solutions, and support implementation while maintaining usability and design consistency.

Outcome

Delivered iterative UX improvements efficiently within agile workflows while balancing speed, technical feasibility, and product quality.

04 / 04

Internal Operational Dashboard

Fraud Detection & Risk Monitoring (OFM FDR)

Problem

Backend operational teams needed a clearer and more efficient way to monitor fraud activity, risk scoring, and security controls across the platform.

What I Did

Designed an internal dashboard experience focused on improving visibility of operational data, surfacing key risk indicators, and supporting faster decision-making for internal teams.

Outcome

Improved clarity and accessibility of complex operational information, helping teams monitor and respond to risks more effectively.

What it added up to

Due to NDA constraints, detailed metrics and product visuals cannot be shared publicly.

It also strengthened my ability to work iteratively within agile teams, balancing speed, usability, and delivery realities while continuously improving live product experiences.
